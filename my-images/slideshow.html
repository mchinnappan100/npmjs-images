Keyboard & Feature Guide

Navigation
←→ Previous / next image
Prev Next On-screen buttons for the same
Click thumbnail Jump directly to any image via the thumbnail strip
Playback
Space Play / Pause the auto-advance slideshow
Play button Same as Space — starts or stops the auto-timer
Fullscreen
F Enter fullscreen — image takes over the entire screen, all chrome hidden
F11 Also enters fullscreen (browser shortcut intercepted)
ESC or F Exit fullscreen and return to normal view
Mouse move Reveals the floating controls bar at the top of the fullscreen overlay (auto-hides after 3 s)
Thumbnail strip (fullscreen only)
T Toggle the slide-up thumbnail strip at the bottom of the fullscreen overlay
Thumbnails btn Same as T — also available in the fullscreen controls bar
Click thumb Jump to that image while staying in fullscreen; active image is highlighted in blue
Folder browser
D Open / close the folder picker panel
Folders button Same as D — shows all sub-folders with a preview thumbnail and image count
Click folder Instantly switches the slideshow to images in that folder
Help panel
? Open / close this help panel from anywhere
Help button Same as ? — in the main controls bar
ESC Close this panel (when fullscreen is not active)
